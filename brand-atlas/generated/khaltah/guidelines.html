Image-generated brand concept

Khaltah خلطة

Vision

A future where unexpected Saudi products emerge from disciplines that rarely share the same table. Khaltah would assemble technologists, storytellers, designers, and cultural thinkers around a clear idea, then tune the mix until it becomes coherent and useful.

مستقبل تظهر فيه منتجات سعودية غير متوقعة من تخصصات قلما تجتمع حول طاولة واحدة. تجمع خلطة التقنيين والكتّاب والمصممين والمفكرين الثقافيين حول فكرة واضحة، ثم تضبط المزيج حتى يصبح متماسكًا ونافعًا.

Brand approach

Build Khaltah as a disciplined creative blend with a lively social edge. Distinct ingredients, bold color relationships, and playful precision show that surprising outcomes come from considered composition.

تُبنى خلطة كمزيج إبداعي منضبط بطابع اجتماعي حي. تظهر المكونات المتمايزة والعلاقات اللونية الجريئة والدقة المرحة أن النتائج المفاجئة تأتي من تركيب مدروس.

Usage

Keep the generated logo proportions. Use a clear area equal to at least the symbol height around the logo. Retain the original files and review legibility at the actual intended size. The raster logo is a concept; final production needs vector redraw, responsive small-size variants, Arabic proofreading and print specifications.

What is included

Three built-in image-generator originals: transparent logo exploration, identity application world, full-page landing concept. Eight individual application layouts in editable HTML, PNG and WebP. A responsive working landing page, English/Arabic copy, palette tokens, generation briefs and provenance records and font licenses.

Strengths

  • Strong multiproduct invention story
  • Vibrant playful visual language
  • Human and memorable
  • Appeals to creative/experimental audiences

Weaknesses & watchouts

  • Food/recipe associations
  • Potentially insufficiently corporate
  • May feel random without rigorous brand system
  • English transliteration variance

Provenance

The three originals were generated with the built-in image tool. Individual applications were rendered from HTML with that generated logo. Working landing HTML is an interpretation of the brand strategy and generated world, not a pixel-for-pixel reconstruction of the image concept.
