Wameed وميض

Vision

A future where Saudi enterprises can read complex operations as clearly as a live signal, then act before delays compound. Wameed would turn scattered data into focused intelligence that helps leaders make sound decisions and teams move with shared confidence.

مستقبل تقرأ فيه المؤسسات السعودية عملياتها المعقدة بوضوح الإشارة الحية، وتتحرك قبل أن تتراكم التأخيرات. يحوّل وميض البيانات المتفرقة إلى معرفة مركزة تساعد القادة على اتخاذ قرارات سليمة، والفرق على العمل بثقة مشتركة.

Brand approach

Position Wameed as the composed intelligence layer for serious operators. A restrained identity and signal-led language make sophisticated analysis feel legible, immediate, and ready for the decisions that follow.

يتموضع وميض بوصفه طبقة ذكاء رزينة للمشغلين الجادين. تجعل الهوية الهادئة واللغة المستوحاة من الإشارة التحليل المتقدم واضحًا وفوريًا، ومهيأً للقرار التالي.

Usage

Keep the generated logo proportions. Use a clear area equal to at least the symbol height around the logo. Retain the original files and review legibility at the actual intended size. The raster logo is a concept; final production needs vector redraw, responsive small-size variants, Arabic proofreading and print specifications.

What is included

Three built-in image-generator originals: transparent logo exploration, identity application world, full-page landing concept. Eight individual application layouts in editable HTML, PNG and WebP. A responsive working landing page, English/Arabic copy, palette tokens, generation briefs and provenance records and font licenses.
