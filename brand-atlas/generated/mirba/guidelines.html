Mirba' مرباع

Vision

A future where Saudi-rooted ventures grow from sustained exchange between local knowledge, technology, and global ambition. Mirba’ would create a shared home for makers and institutions to gather, shape opportunities, and build companies with relevance beyond one market.

مستقبل تنمو فيه مشاريع ذات جذور سعودية من تبادل مستمر بين المعرفة المحلية والتقنية والطموح العالمي. يهيئ مرباع بيتًا مشتركًا يجتمع فيه الصناع والمؤسسات لصياغة الفرص وبناء شركات تتجاوز صلتها سوقًا واحدة.

Brand approach

Shape Mirba’ as an architectural gathering point for venture creation. A central void, modular structure, and cultured restraint support a rooted global outlook without asserting an unverified modern meaning.

يتشكل مرباع كنقطة اجتماع معمارية لصناعة المشاريع. يدعم الفراغ المركزي والبنية المعيارية والهدوء الثقافي أفقًا عالميًا ذا جذور، من دون الجزم بمعنى حديث غير موثق.

Usage

Keep the generated logo proportions. Use a clear area equal to at least the symbol height around the logo. Retain the original files and review legibility at the actual intended size. The raster logo is a concept; final production needs vector redraw, responsive small-size variants, Arabic proofreading and print specifications.

What is included

Three built-in image-generator originals: transparent logo exploration, identity application world, full-page landing concept. Eight individual application layouts in editable HTML, PNG and WebP. A responsive working landing page, English/Arabic copy, palette tokens, generation briefs and provenance records and font licenses.

Strengths

  • Potential Saudi-rooted distinctiveness
  • Architecture and shared space visual story
  • Premium long-term venture narrative
  • Could bridge culture and advanced products

Weaknesses & watchouts

  • Dictionary records historic quarter-of-spoils meaning
  • Modern local interpretation not verified
  • English pronunciation/spelling hard
  • May be obscure to customers
