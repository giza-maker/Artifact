Sanni سنّع

Vision

A future where growing operations remain coherent as more people, tools, and services join them. Sanni would help organizations shape practical systems that feel orderly to use, easier to maintain, and ready for responsible expansion.

مستقبل تظل فيه العمليات المتنامية متماسكة مع انضمام مزيد من الأشخاص والأدوات والخدمات. يساعد سنّع المؤسسات على تشكيل أنظمة عملية تبدو مرتبة في الاستخدام، أسهل في الصيانة، ومستعدة للتوسع المسؤول.

Brand approach

Build Sanni around the satisfaction of putting work in order. Balanced structures, polished connections, and plainspoken guidance give operational design a capable human character without hiding complexity.

تُبنى سنّع حول متعة وضع العمل في نصابه. تمنح البنى المتوازنة والوصلات المصقولة والإرشاد المباشر التصميم التشغيلي طابعًا إنسانيًا متمكنًا، من دون إخفاء التعقيد.

Usage

Keep the generated logo proportions. Use a clear area equal to at least the symbol height around the logo. Retain the original files and review legibility at the actual intended size. The raster logo is a concept; final production needs vector redraw, responsive small-size variants, Arabic proofreading and print specifications.

What is included

Three built-in image-generator originals: transparent logo exploration, identity application world, full-page landing concept. Eight individual application layouts in editable HTML, PNG and WebP. A responsive working landing page, English/Arabic copy, palette tokens, generation briefs and provenance records and font licenses.
