First-Time Buyers

Your Path to
Ownership in 6 Steps

Buying your first home in a Silicon Valley market feels overwhelming. We've closed over 340 deals in Mountain View — here's the exact process we walk you through.

1

Get Pre-Approved (Not Just Pre-Qualified)

A pre-approval letter from a local lender carries far more weight in competitive offers than a pre-qualification. We'll connect you with Mountain View lenders who know the market and can turn underwriting around in 24 hours.

2

Define Your Non-Negotiables

Schools, commute to your employer, garage vs. parking, outdoor space — we'll map your priorities against what's realistically available in your budget before you fall in love with the wrong property.

3

Tour With Strategy

We don't just tour open houses. We preview properties before they hit Zillow, track off-market opportunities, and brief you on each seller's motivation before you walk in the door.

4

Craft a Winning Offer

In Mountain View, how you structure an offer matters as much as the price. Escalation clauses, cover letters, flexible closing timelines — we'll build the package that wins without overpaying.

5

Inspection and Due Diligence

We coordinate with the best local inspectors — structural, pest, roof, foundation. You'll have a complete picture before removing contingencies. No surprises at closing.

6

Close and Move In

We manage the escrow timeline, coordinate with lenders and title, and make sure you're handing keys by your target date. October closings qualify for Q4 tax benefits — we'll remind you of every deduction.

Neighborhoods

Mountain View's distinct neighborhoods each offer a different lifestyle — from walkable downtown living to quiet tree-lined cul-de-sacs near top-rated schools.

Downtown / Castro Street
Most Walkable

Mountain View's most vibrant corridor. Restaurants, farmers markets, Caltrain access, and year-round street life — ideal for tech professionals who want city energy without SF rent.

Median: $1.62M / 1–2 bed condos popular
Cuesta Park
Best for Families

Quiet, tree-lined streets with top MVWSD schools, a beloved neighborhood park, and single-family homes with yards. The most sought-after neighborhood for first-time buyers with young children.

Median: $2.05M / SFR 3–4 bd
Rex Manor / Shoreline West
Best Entry Price

Mountain View's most accessible neighborhood for first-time buyers. Proximity to Google HQ and Shoreline Amphitheatre, with post-war ranch homes that offer renovation upside.

Median: $1.58M / 3 bd ranch homes
Old Mountain View
Historic Charm

Victorian and Craftsman homes within walking distance of downtown and Caltrain. The most characterful stock in the city, popular with buyers who want a neighborhood with architectural soul.

Median: $1.78M / Craftsman & Victorian
