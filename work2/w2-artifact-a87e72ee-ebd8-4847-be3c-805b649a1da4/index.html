Compliance Checklist

NY Licensing & Regulations

Everything you must have in place before your first sale. No launch-blocking gaps identified — all have clear resolution paths.

🏛️
NYS Department of Agriculture & Markets
  • Grade A Pasteurized Milk License (Form DAM-101) — required before any sales
  • Dairy Dealer License — for purchasing/reselling Grade A milk
  • Annual farm and facility inspection compliance
  • Cold storage certified at ≤45°F throughout supply chain
Estimated cost: $150–$300/year · Apply at: agriculture.ny.gov
🏙️
NYC Department of Health & Mental Hygiene
  • Retail Food Store License (if operating a physical location in NYC)
  • Food Protection Certificate for responsible party
  • HACCP plan for cold storage and distribution
  • Annual permit renewal and inspections
Estimated cost: $280–$400/year · Apply at: nyc.gov/health
📋
FDA Labeling Requirements (21 CFR 101)
  • Nutrition Facts panel required on all retail packages
  • Ingredient list and allergen declarations
  • Net quantity of contents statement
  • "Pasteurized" statement on all fluid milk products
  • Grade A shield if applicable
Label design cost: $200–$600 (one-time)
🚚
Cold Chain & Distribution Compliance
  • Refrigerated transport required — maintained at ≤45°F at all times
  • Temperature log documentation for all deliveries
  • Vehicle inspected and certified for food transport
  • Sell-by date compliance — typically 14–21 days from processing
Refrigerated van lease: ~$800–$1,500/month if self-delivering
