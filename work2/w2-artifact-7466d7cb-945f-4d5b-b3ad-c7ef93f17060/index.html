Rules priority

How to play without ruffling feathers.

Duck Deck is easy to explain: collect useful cards, dodge ridiculous pond problems, and out-quack your friends before the final flap.

Start with Quick Rules ↓
  1. 1 Deal five cards. Every player gets a starting hand and one dramatic duck face.
  2. 2 Play one card per turn. Build your flock, trigger pond effects, or sabotage a rival nest.
  3. 3 Score when the deck runs dry. Biggest, weirdest, best-managed flock wins.
Objective: build the boldest flock
  • Collect duck cards to grow your flock score.
  • Use action cards to swap, shield, steal, or cause harmless pond mayhem.
  • Win by having the highest flock score when the draw deck is empty.
Turn flow: draw, play, quack
  • Draw one card from the deck.
  • Play one card from your hand, then resolve its effect.
  • Discard down to seven cards if your hand gets too splashy.
Special cards: pond events and duck tricks
  • Pond Event: affects all players for one round.
  • Duck Trick: creates a one-time surprise like a swap or shield.
  • Bread Crumb Bonus: adds points, but may attract chaos.
Winning: count the flock, crown the quack
  • Add visible duck values, bonuses, and end-game effects.
  • Break ties by most Duck Trick cards played.
  • If still tied, the player with the best duck impression wins the moral victory.
