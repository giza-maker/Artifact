Production Methodology

How we
make the films.

Each Grimoire production follows a rigorous six-phase pipeline — from IP verification through to financier-ready package delivery. Every phase is owned by a specialist agent and reviewed at the gate before proceeding.

01
Phase One
IP Verification & Rights Assessment

Dr. Marsh confirms public domain status, original production history, and any active trademark considerations before development begins.

02
Phase Two
Adaptation Bible

Constantine Weir builds the full development document: themes, structure, character arc, and adaptation philosophy. This locks before screenwriting begins.

03
Phase Three
Visual Development

Mira Halcyon produces reference packages: creature design, environment, tone, and period aesthetic. Delivered as briefing documents for physical production.

04
Phase Four
Screenplay

Petra Vane writes the full feature screenplay in professional format. Draft iteration continues until the story passes all six acceptance criteria from Development.

05
Phase Five
Pre-Production Package

Aldous Frame assembles the full pre-production planning document: schedule, location breakdown, department briefs, and budget framework.

06
Phase Six
Campaign & Distribution

Vivienne Stark builds the investor pitch deck, marketing strategy, and festival positioning. The studio package is complete and ready for human production partners.

Why AI-native production?

The classic monster film requires vast historical and technical knowledge, deep IP research, and iterative creative development — all areas where AI specialist agents can move faster and deeper than traditional development.

Classic IP Depth
Our agents carry comprehensive knowledge of Universal Monsters production history, director filmographies, and original screenplay archives — without the limitations of human research speed.
Iterative Development
Screenplay iteration, visual reference generation, and structural analysis all run concurrently across departments — compressing the typical 18-month development cycle to weeks.
Zero Placeholder Policy
Grimoire operates under a hard constraint: no placeholder content, no fabricated credits, no invented statistics. Every claim in every document is verified by Research before it ships.
Human Production Ready
Grimoire's output is designed to be handed directly to a human production company. We produce the development package, not the film. The film is yours to make.

AI Infrastructure

What powers
the studio.

Grimoire Pictures is built on a multi-agent architecture — specialist models coordinated by a studio-level orchestration layer that manages handoffs, quality gates, and version control across all productions simultaneously.

🧠
Multi-Agent Architecture

Six permanent specialist agents — each trained on domain-specific corpora — coordinated by a studio orchestration layer. Agents collaborate asynchronously with defined handoff gates between phases.

📜
Persistent Production Memory

Each production builds a persistent knowledge base across its lifecycle — character bibles, research notes, visual references, and screenplay drafts persist and are accessible to all agents throughout development.

🔍
IP Verification Engine

The Research department runs automated IP status checks against multiple public domain databases before any production advances. No work begins on contested or ambiguous IP.

✦
Zero Placeholder Enforcement

A hardcoded quality gate prevents any document containing placeholder text, fabricated credits, or unverified claims from advancing to the next development phase. All outputs are verified before delivery.

🎬
Professional Format Standards

Screenplays output in strict industry-standard format. Pre-production documents follow AICP and PACT conventions. All creative deliverables are production-house compatible on day one.

🤝
Human Production Interface

Every Grimoire output is designed for direct handoff to human production partners — no translation layer required. The studio speaks the language of physical film production.
