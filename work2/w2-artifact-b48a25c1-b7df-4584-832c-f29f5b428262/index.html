// 07 — Voice & Tone
Brand Voice
@PULSE's voice is what makes it unmistakable. Urgent, direct, and never boring — but always defensible.
🔥
The PULSE Voice
  • Urgent but never panicked — calm confidence under pressure
  • Contrarian when warranted, never just for attention
  • Short sentences. White space. No padding.
  • Always adds context: "this matters because..."
  • One take per tweet — no hedging, no "both sides"
  • Willing to be wrong, fast to update when corrected
🎯
Content Personality
  • Thinks like a journalist, writes like a founder
  • Treats the audience as intelligent — never condescending
  • Finds the second-order implication most miss
  • Engages with replies substantively — not just likes
  • Personal but not personal — life is context, not content
  • Emoji: ⚡🔥📡🎙️ — your 4 signatures only
✓ Do Write
  • ⚡"OpenAI's valuation isn't about revenue. It's about controlling the next OS. Everyone's looking at the wrong metric."
  • ⚡"The VC market isn't frozen. It's bifurcated. If you're building AI infra, money is flowing. Everything else is fighting for scraps."
  • ⚡"Three things happened today that most people missed. Here's why they matter more than the headline everyone's talking about 🧵"
✗ Don't Write
  • ❌"Interesting thread! Some good points here, definitely food for thought. What do you think everyone?"
  • ❌"I think maybe possibly the market could potentially go either way, but it's hard to say for sure..."
  • ❌"BREAKING BREAKING BREAKING: [thing that happened 4 hours ago that everyone already covered]"
