// The Formula

Viral Mechanics

The four forces that make content spread. Understand these and you can engineer virality on demand.

🎣
The Hook Formula
The first 1-2 lines determine 90% of your reach. On Twitter, readers decide in 0.3 seconds. Your hook must create a curiosity gap that physically can't be closed without reading more.
WEAK: "Here are some thoughts on AI"
STRONG: "AI is about to make 60% of your team redundant. Not in 10 years. This quarter."

Rule: State the most alarming / counterintuitive version of the truth.
💡
The Contrast Engine
The fastest path to virality is contrasting what EVERYONE believes against what's actually true. Contrarian takes that can be defended are the highest-engagement content format on all of Twitter.
"[Popular belief] is wrong."
"Here's what's actually happening: [reframe]"
"This matters because: [implication]"

Never say "I think". Assert. Be willing to be wrong.
⚡
Timing & Momentum
Posting during breaking news moments 10x's your reach. Algorithm rewards accounts that post about trending topics early and add new analysis, not just regurgitation.
Best posting windows:
7:00–9:00 AM ET (morning commute)
12:00–1:00 PM ET (lunch scroll)
4:00–6:00 PM ET (end of workday)

Break-news post window: within 8 minutes.
🔁
Retweet Engineering
Write posts that other accounts WANT to share because sharing it makes THEM look smart. The easiest way to engineer retweets is to make your content a status signal for the retweeter.
Ask yourself before posting:
"Would someone share this to signal they're smart?"
"Does this make the retweeter look informed?"

If yes → post. If no → rewrite the framing.
