Flagship Collection · 2026

Verdant
Sparkling Tea

Single-origin · Natural carbonation · No alcohol · Wine service format · 750ml and 375ml

"What if the most interesting thing on the table wasn't the wine?"

Sparkling Tea is the first product in the Verdant Provisions catalog — and the most important category we will ever launch. We believe the non-alcoholic beverage program at fine dining restaurants is a decade behind the food, and Sparkling Tea is the product that closes that gap.

Unlike commercial non-alcoholic sparkling beverages, our range is sourced from single-estate gardens across Japan, Taiwan, Darjeeling, and Kenya. Each tea is processed to preserve varietal character, then naturally carbonated using a secondary fermentation method that mirrors traditional méthode champenoise — without producing alcohol.

Presented in 750ml Burgundy-format bottles with hand-applied labels, it belongs in the sommelier's hand, not behind the bar. It commands a price point between $22 and $38 per bottle wholesale — and supports a $14–$22 by-the-glass programme.

The Distribution Model

Direct to kitchen.
White-glove from start
to service.

Verdant Provisions is a direct-distribution company. No intermediaries. No broadline distributors. Your sommelier speaks directly to our team — and we speak directly to the producer.

01
Partnership
Restaurant Qualification

We evaluate each partner restaurant against our placement criteria — menu philosophy, service standards, and guest profile. We accept a limited number of new partners per quarter.

02
Onboarding
Tasting & Menu Integration

Our team conducts an in-house tasting with the sommelier and executive chef. We co-develop the placement strategy — by-the-glass, pairing programme, or feature position — and provide full staff training materials.

03
Delivery
Weekly Direct Delivery

Climate-controlled delivery to your back-of-house on a weekly schedule. Minimum order 1 case per varietal. No standing orders required — you order what you need, when you need it.

04
Support
Ongoing Account Management

A dedicated account manager for every partner. Quarterly new product previews, exclusive allocations on limited releases, and direct access to producer stories for your front-of-house team.

Partner Restaurant Criteria

  • Full-service fine dining — tasting menu or à la carte prix-fixe preferred
  • Dedicated sommelier or beverage director on staff
  • Operating in a major US metropolitan market (NYC, LA, SF, Chicago, Miami, Houston, Boston, DC)
  • Minimum 50 covers per service
  • Commitment to genuine menu integration — not back-bar storage
  • Willingness to educate front-of-house staff on product provenance
  • No exclusivity required — we work alongside your existing wine and beverage programme

We evaluate each restaurant individually. If you don't meet every criterion but believe your programme is the right fit, apply anyway — we review all submissions personally.
