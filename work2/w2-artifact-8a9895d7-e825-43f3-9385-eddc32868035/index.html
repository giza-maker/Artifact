Meet the Animals

Over 6,000 animals from 400+ species call Giza Zoo home — from the mighty Nile crocodile to the graceful African giraffe.

🦁
African Lion
Panthera leo
The king of the savanna now roams a brand-new 2,000m² habitat with naturalistic pride rock formations and enrichment activities daily.
🌍 Sub-Saharan Africa
🐘
African Elephant
Loxodonta africana
Our herd of three enjoys a spacious 5-hectare habitat complete with a mud wallow, foraging area, and daily keeper talks at 10AM and 3PM.
🌍 East Africa
🦒
Reticulated Giraffe
Giraffa reticulata
Stretch up and hand-feed our gentle giants from the elevated giraffe deck — one of the most popular experiences at the new Giza Zoo.
🌍 Northern Kenya
🐊
Nile Crocodile
Crocodylus niloticus
Egypt's ancient reptile, once worshipped as the god Sobek. Watch them bask in the new Nile Habitat — a living connection to Egypt's history.
🌊 Nile River Basin
🦓
Plains Zebra
Equus quagga
No two zebras have the same stripe pattern — spot the differences in our beautiful herd living in the newly redesigned savanna zone.
🌍 Eastern Africa
🦩
Greater Flamingo
Phoenicopterus roseus
Hundreds of elegant flamingos colour the new Wetlands Lagoon pink — a breathtaking spectacle and perfect for photography.
🌊 Nile Delta & Africa

// Opening Day Programme

Grand Opening Events

A full day of celebration, discovery, and wonder for the whole family. Here's what's happening on 15th May.

⭐ Headline Event
📅 15 May 2026 · 9:00 AM
🎊
The Grand Reopening Ceremony
Join Egypt's Minister of Environment, the Mayor of Giza, and our conservation partners for the official ribbon-cutting ceremony. Live traditional music, a parade of animal ambassadors, and a spectacular balloon release mark the start of a new era for Giza Zoo.
🕘 9:00 AM – 10:30 AM  ·  Main Entrance Plaza
📅 Opening Day
🦁
Lion Habitat Unveiling
First public viewing of the brand-new 2,000m² lion enclosure, designed by leading habitat architects with naturalistic pride rock and live prey simulation enrichment.
🕙 10:30 AM – 12:00 PM  ·  African Savanna Zone
📅 Opening Day
🎨
Kids' Conservation Workshop
Interactive workshop teaching children about wildlife conservation, animal tracking, and Egypt's native species. Each child receives a conservation passport to stamp throughout the zoo.
🕛 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM  ·  Education Centre
📅 Opening Day
🦒
Giraffe Feeding Experience
Meet our giraffe herd up close on the elevated deck. Keepers guide families through safe hand-feeding with acacia leaves. Booking required — limited spots available.
🕒 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM  ·  Giraffe Deck
📅 Opening Day
🎆
Sunset Celebration & Fireworks
Close out the grand opening day with a spectacular sunset gathering at the main lake, live Egyptian folk performances, and a fireworks finale visible across Giza.
🌅 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM  ·  Lake Amphitheatre
