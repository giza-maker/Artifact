# Nomli Android demo APK

Installable Android demo of Nomli's focused kitchen prototype.

- Kitchen inventory, shopping and grocery spending.
- Five scanning simulations with editable review.
- Recipe matching, guided cooking, timers and reviewed ingredient use.
- Contextual scripted Zara and sample voice transcript review.
- Native camera/photo selection and barcode capture tools included for preview.

Version: 0.1.0-demo. Package: com.nomli.mvp. Minimum Android API: 26.

Live OCR, product lookup, voice transcription, LLM responses and cloud storage are not connected. This is a debug-signed demo. Physical-device testing remains pending.
