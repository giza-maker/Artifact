# Nomli investor demonstration guide

Prepared 24 October 2026 for the 25 October meeting. Functional rehearsals are not a substitute for the presenter's timed rehearsal on the meeting laptop.

## Before presenting

1. Open `app/index.html?investor=1` from a local server or the live site.
2. Choose Reset → Reset sample session.
3. Confirm the starting state: 440 XP, no completed lesson, no reviewed scan, no imported receipt and no shared meal.
4. Keep default fictional preferences for the short path. Wheat exclusions use the compatible tomato salad instead of bruschetta.
5. Keep the offline folder available. The page never asks for an email address or claims a working lead form.

## Four-minute talk track

| Time      | Action                                                            | Show and say                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0:00–0:25 | Show the landing hero or Home                                     | Learn cooking skills, manage the kitchen and enjoy the progress. This is an interactive prototype using sample data.                                                                             |
| 0:25–0:50 | Continue tour → use sample profile                                | Confidence, goals, household and time shape the experience. Five ingredients create first value without a full pantry setup.                                                                     |
| 0:50–1:20 | Review scan → confirm dates                                       | Scanning is simulated. Users review quantities and uncertain dates. Tools and storage areas are part of Kitchen.                                                                                 |
| 1:20–2:00 | Open salt lesson → read → correct knowledge check                 | Chef School teaches a concrete reason. The learning measure is separate from XP.                                                                                                                 |

| 2:45–3:20 | Apply consumption → collect reward                                | Inventory changes; the lesson reaches 3/3 guided milestones. 440 + 40 recipe XP + 20 technique XP = 500. Heat-control challenge opens. These are self-reported milestones, not verified mastery. |
| 3:20–3:40 | Continue to share meal                                            | The illustrative meal appears in the local sample Hub. No public post is sent.                                                                                                                   |
| 3:40–4:00 | Show validation plan                                              | Prototype built. Funding would enable an MVP, evaluated AI/safety and the 90-day usability/value/commercial gates. State the founder-approved ask; no raise amount is invented in the mockup.    |

## Optional branches after the core journey

- Kitchen: storage filters, add/edit ingredients, consumed/discarded corrections; switch off the toaster and inspect the adapted recipe method.
- Expenses: paper and digital receipt samples; edit quantity, name, combined-stock date or line total; confirm and save once. Category, weekly and monthly totals update together. No real OCR or uploads.
- Budget: enter a positive amount with up to two decimals; show remaining/over-budget state.
- Play: accept the three-action weekly challenge; claim its 30 XP once after learning, cooking and sharing. Explain seeded flexible-week examples.
- Hub: local Cheer, Reply, Save, Follow and Report/Block examples.
- Future features: family, voice/languages, meal planning, offline syncing, accounts/privacy, subscriptions and ordering are labeled previews.

## Honest answers

- Built: the linked, responsive prototype and simulated journeys.
- Not connected: production AI, OCR, accounts, payments, merchant ordering, authentic community or moderation.
- Learning: guided practice and knowledge checks; no certification or independently verified skill.
- Plans: three free starter lessons; later capabilities and commercial packaging require validation.
- Targets: stretch scenarios with explicit math, not forecasts or traction.
- Contact: intentionally unset. Use the founder's own meeting follow-up process.

## Presenter checks still required

Have another person explain the three pillars without coaching. Rehearse the talk track twice on the actual presentation browser/display and confirm the funding ask and ownership. A real-time narrated screen recording is a separate backup item; do not describe the screenshot set as a recording.
