Nomli
Investor product brief · 25 October 2026

One everyday loop for learning, running and enjoying your kitchen.

Nomli tests whether cooking guidance becomes more useful when it shares context with kitchen management and progress that gives people a reason to return.

01 / Learn

Skills in context

Chef Zara, three starter lessons, knowledge checks and guided recipe practice connect technique to tonight's cooking. Practice is self-reported; Nomli does not claim certification or verified mastery.

02 / Manage

The whole kitchen

Ingredients, storage, equipment, recipes and shopping inform the next action. Reviewed sample receipts update inventory and grocery spending together; budgets and category totals stay visible.

03 / Play

Meaningful progress

Ingredient quests, learning quests, weekly challenges, levels and achievements link progress to cooking activity. Food Hub and all community activity shown are illustrative.

Built now

Responsive interactive prototype with fixed sample data. Linked Learn, Manage and Play journeys support editable inventory, lessons, recipes, quests, sample receipt review and reconciled spending.

Simulated or unproven

Camera scanning, OCR, AI coaching, receipt extraction, community posts and payments are simulated. No production users, revenue, traction, live integrations or independently validated learning outcome is claimed.

Five-minute meeting path

  1. Start at 440 XP and state the prototype boundary.
  2. Manage: review kitchen stock and a simulated scan.
  3. Learn: complete a technique lesson and cook.
  4. Play: see milestones, collect eligible XP and share an illustrative meal.
  5. Optional: review a sample receipt; show inventory and spending update once.

Evidence before scale

Days 1-30: Can target users understand and complete the core loop?

Days 31-60: Do they return, cook and practice without prompting?

Days 61-90: Which package earns willingness to pay?

Funding conversation: define a 90-day MVP validation mandate. Capital would fund MVP delivery, evaluated AI and safety controls, and a controlled pilot. Raise amount, terms, team plan and milestones require founder approval; none are invented here.
