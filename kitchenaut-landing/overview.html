Product overview · October 2026

Learn to cook.
Manage your kitchen.
Enjoy the progress.

A connected cooking experience: use what you own, learn a practical technique and build a habit worth repeating.

01 / Learn

Skills in context

Chef Zara, three free starter lessons and guided recipe practice. Learning milestones are separate from activity XP.

02 / Manage

The whole kitchen

Ingredients, storage, equipment, recipes and shopping. Receipts, grocery budgets and spending form an optional branch.

03 / Play

Meaningful progress

Ingredient and skill quests, levels, challenges, achievements and an illustrative Food Hub connect effort to progress.

Current stage: responsive interactive prototype with sample data. Coaching, scanning, receipt extraction and community activity are simulated. No production users, revenue, AI integration, billing or certified skill is claimed.

The demonstration

  1. Set a sample cooking profile.
  2. Review ingredients and dates.
  3. Learn and check a technique.
  4. Cook and confirm inventory use.
  5. See skill milestones and collect XP.
  6. Share an illustrative meal.

The next validation

Days 1–30: comprehension and task usability.

Days 31–60: repeat value and learning behavior.

Days 61–90: packaging and willingness to pay.

Funding discussion: MVP delivery, evaluated safety/AI and staged validation. Scope and raise amount require founder agreement.
