# Nomli investor meeting checklist

Prepared 24 October 2026 for the 25 October meeting.

## Founder decisions before the meeting

- [ ] Name meeting owner, presenter and note-taker.
- [ ] Confirm audience, meeting length and desired next step.
- [ ] Approve one sentence on problem and one sentence on why Nomli now.
- [ ] Approve funding discussion: 90-day MVP validation mandate, or a specific raise only if amount, use, runway and terms are real.
- [ ] Confirm team, ownership, traction and partnership claims. Remove any claim without evidence.
- [ ] Add a real follow-up contact or keep contact intentionally unset.

## Evidence pack

- [ ] Open `overview.html`; print one copy per attendee plus two spares.
- [ ] Keep `INVESTOR_GUIDE.md` available for presenter notes.
- [ ] Prepare sourced user, market and competitor evidence. Do not present assumptions as research.
- [ ] Prepare simple 90-day MVP budget and milestone plan. Mark every estimate.
- [ ] List top five risks: adoption, repeat use, learning validity, recognition accuracy and food-safety boundaries.
- [ ] Prepare explicit answer for what is built, simulated, planned and still undecided.

## Demo readiness

- [ ] Use actual meeting laptop, browser, display, power adapter and clicker.
- [ ] Open `app/index.html?investor=1` from live site or local static server.
- [ ] Reset. Confirm 440 XP, no completed lesson, no reviewed scan, no imported receipt and no shared meal.
- [ ] Rehearse core route twice: Manage -> Learn -> Play.
- [ ] Rehearse optional receipt branch: sample capture -> editable review -> save once -> inventory and spending update.
- [ ] Verify receipt duplicate protection and sample-SAR disclosure.
- [ ] Prepare offline local package and screenshots as fallback. Do not call screenshots a recording.

## Say clearly

- [ ] "This is an interactive prototype using fixed sample data."
- [ ] "Scanning, OCR, AI coaching, community activity and payments are simulated."
- [ ] "Guided practice is self-reported. We do not claim certification or verified mastery."
- [ ] "This prototype does not provide evidence of production users, revenue or validated traction." Present any separate verified evidence explicitly.
- [ ] "Next step is a controlled MVP test with measurable 30-, 60- and 90-day gates."

## Five-minute route

- [ ] 0:00-0:25 - frame Learn, Manage and Play as equal parts of one loop.
- [ ] 0:25-1:35 - Manage: kitchen stock and reviewed sample scan.
- [ ] 1:35-2:50 - Learn: technique, knowledge check and guided cooking.
- [ ] 2:50-3:50 - Play: milestone, XP, quest and illustrative sharing.
- [ ] 3:50-4:30 - optional receipt and spending reconciliation.
- [ ] 4:30-5:00 - prototype boundary, 90-day evidence plan and next-step request.

## Immediately after

- [ ] Record objections, unanswered questions and exact follow-up owner.
- [ ] Send only material used in meeting; label estimates and simulations.
- [ ] Schedule agreed next step and deadline.
- [ ] Convert investor feedback into testable assumptions before changing roadmap.
