01The problem

Recipe apps are passive. They don't motivate, teach or reward.

Meanwhile, the waste is large, and most of it happens at home.

  • 1.05billion tonnes

    of food wasted in 2022, which is 19% of the food available to consumers.

  • 631million tonnes

    came from households, about 60% of the total.

  • 1bn+meals a day

    Households throw out over 1 billion meals' worth of food every day.

  • 8–10%of global emissions

    of global greenhouse gas emissions come from food loss and waste.

Source: UN Environment Programme, Food Waste Index Report 2024 (opens in a new tab).

  • Recipes don't motivate

    Daily quests turn what is expiring into today's mission.

  • Recipes don't teach

    Chef Zara and Chef School coach real technique.

  • Recipes don't reward

    XP, streaks and badges reward the habit.

02How it works

Scan. Quest. Cook. Share. Level up.

Five connected steps. Each one feeds the next, and the last sends you back to the start.

  1. Step 1

    Scan

    Add what you own, with expiry dates: fridge, freezer, pantry, spice rack, even your pans.

  2. Step 2

    Quest

    Whatever is about to expire becomes today's mission.

  3. Step 3

    Cook

    Chef Zara coaches you through it, step by step.

  4. Step 4

    Share

    Post the dish you cooked to the Food Hub.

  5. Step 5

    Level up

    Earn XP, keep your streak, unlock the next level.

Then tomorrow's scan starts the loop again.

              04The product

              Five modules. One daily habit.

              Every screen below comes from the interactive prototype. Each module is planned for the phase shown.

              4.1Fridge

              See everything you own, and when it expires.

              Scan your fridge, freezer, pantry and spice rack. Barcode lookup and expiry-date reading come first; produce recognition and receipt scanning follow in later phases.

              Alerts arrive 48 and 24 hours before anything expires. It also keeps a list of your pots, pans and appliances, so recipes only ask for tools you own.

              05Progression

              Cooking that levels you up.

              XP, streaks and badges give every dish a reason to happen again.

              XP

              Earn experience points for cooking real dishes, with bonuses for good technique.

              Streaks

              Cook on consecutive days to keep your streak alive. The habit is the point.

              Badges

              Badges mark milestones, all the way up to the Culinary Master mentor badge.
