Product thesis

Cooking support is split across three incomplete experiences.

Learn

Instructions rarely build skill

Recipes finish one meal but often miss the technique that transfers to the next.

Manage

Kitchen context lives elsewhere

Ingredients, expiry, tools, budgets and shopping lists sit in separate workflows.

Play

Good intentions lack a return loop

Most utilities stop at the answer and do little to sustain practice.

One connected loop

Each pillar makes the other two stronger.

Kitchen context shapes the recipe. The recipe becomes a lesson and quest. Completion updates skills and inventory, improving the next session.

  1. 1Set skill goals
  2. 2Confirm kitchen
  3. 3Match meal + technique
  4. 4Cook with guidance
  5. 5Update progress

Unvalidated stretch scenario

Scale arithmetic, not a forecast

500K MAU × 8% paid = 40K payers. $3M ARR then requires $6.25 monthly revenue per payer. A 40% Day-30 retention target is also an unvalidated stretch aspiration. Every input needs cohort testing; none represents traction or a forecast.

Investor FAQ

Built now. Validated next.

What exists today?

A responsive sample prototype and investor walkthrough using simulated interactions. It is not a production service.

Where does AI fit?

AI-assisted scan and coaching are directions. This demo does not prove model quality, safety or production integration. Users must confirm food data.

What would funding enable?

An MVP across all three pillars, research, safety, accessibility, instrumentation and the 90-day validation program. No raise amount or team claim is made here.

How would Nomli make money?

Free starter value with paid expansion after capabilities and willingness to pay are validated. Billing follows shipped value.
