Risk Gate

The opportunity is real, but the failure modes are also public: unoriginal-content enforcement, GenAI labeling, deepfake and likeness risk, child-audience quality rules, and unstable payout access.

YouTube

Originality beats automation

YouTube YPP requires eligibility thresholds, policy review, and ongoing compliance. YouTube says Watch Page ads pay 55% of net revenue and Shorts pay 45% of allocated Shorts revenue, but earnings are not guaranteed and final amounts live in YouTube Analytics/AdSense.

YouTube earnings overview

TikTok

Qualified views are narrower than views

TikTok Creator Rewards requires eligible region, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, original content, and videos at least one minute long. AI content can be labeled, but unoriginal or low-quality content can lose rewards.

TikTok Creator Rewards

Instagram

Reach does not imply payout

Instagram monetization requires Partner and Content Monetization Policies. Unoriginal or reproduced content without meaningful enhancement cannot be monetized. Public Reels views do not expose creator payout.
