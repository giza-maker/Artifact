Critical Discovery #1: Plain WireGuard is Blocked by DPI

Standard WireGuard uses a fixed 4-byte header and predictable 148-byte handshake initiation packet. Both Telecom Egypt (AS8452) at Egypt's international border gateway and Gulf carriers (STC, Mobily, e&, du) actively fingerprint and drop plain WireGuard UDP sessions. Our stack therefore locks in AmneziaWG (randomized headers + junk packet padding) as primary, with an automatic 3.5-second fallback to WireGuard-over-WSS (TLS TCP 443).

Critical Discovery #2: How to Beat Law 175 of 2018

Our infrastructure audit revealed that ExpressVPN, Surfshark, and CyberGhost all use Virtual Egypt servers physically hosted in Europe to avoid Egypt's 180-day mandatory data retention law (Law 175/2018, carrying EGP 5–20M fines). We lock in a superior Two-Tier Hybrid Topology: a 10 Gbps Marseille Virtual Egypt hub for streaming/general browsing (zero Egyptian jurisdiction) paired with diskless, RAM-only Cairo ISP relays strictly allowlisted for Egyptian banking & .gov.eg CIDRs.

Both Apple App Store and Google Play prohibit anonymous individual accounts from shipping commercial VPNs, and Egypt's Law 175 of 2018 regulates domestic service providers. Here is the complete compliance blueprint.

Before submitting to Google Play or Apple App Store, the product must be owned by an Offshore Legal Entity (recommended: US Delaware/Wyoming LLC via Stripe Atlas/Firstbase, UK Ltd, or UAE Dubai FZCO) with a verified D-U-N-S Number:

  • Google Play VpnService Policy: Requires an Organization developer account (with D-U-N-S & official business website), a completed VpnService declaration form, and an unedited video demonstrating how the VPN tunnel functions.
  • Google Play "Verified" VPN Badge: Our 6-month trust goal. Requires an independent MASA Level 2 security audit, 10,000+ installs, 250+ reviews, and 90+ days live on Play Store.
  • Jurisdictional Separation: Keeps user accounts, billing (RevenueCat), and support databases strictly outside Egypt.
Architectural Shield · Law 175 of 2018

2. How the Two-Tier Hybrid Mitigates Egyptian Law

Under Egypt's Anti-Cyber and IT Crimes Law (Law 175 of 2018), Article 2 requires domestic "service providers" to retain traffic logs for 180 days, with Article 33 setting fines of EGP 5–20 million. Our architecture mitigates this on three levels:

  • 90%+ Traffic Never Enters an Egyptian VPN Server: Streaming and general browsing exit at our Marseille Virtual Egypt hub in France—outside NTRA and Law 175 territorial jurisdiction.
  • Cairo Relays are Destination-Allowlisted Gateways: Our Cairo Tier 2 nodes only forward encrypted HTTPS packets destined for whitelisted Egyptian bank ASNs and .gov.eg / .com.eg CIDRs. They cannot be used to browse the open web, post on social media, or commit cyber offenses inside Egypt.
  • Zero PII on Cairo Nodes: Cairo relays run diskless in RAM (tmpfs) and authenticate packets via ephemeral cryptographic tokens issued by the offshore control plane.
