## Overview

NileGate presents an Egypt-first hybrid VPN investment thesis through six interactive labs. Preserve the brief's obsidian canvas, papyrus-gold identity and tactile device and network metaphors. Simulations must remain visibly distinct from measured results.

## Colors

Use gold for primary actions and selected states. Reserve green and red for explicit pass, failure and leak meanings, always paired with text or symbols. Keep every act within the same dark surface hierarchy.

## Typography

Use the shared sans stack for interface copy and display headings. Use the mono stack and tabular numerals for prices, packet data and metrics. Balance headings and preserve native Arabic shaping and right-to-left direction inside ad previews.

## Layout

Let the interactive artifact dominate each act. Stack complex layouts on mobile, retaining controls beside their outcomes. Put complete technical records in native disclosures or the decision drawer; keep summaries to three data points and paragraphs below the handover's word limit.

## Elevation & Depth

Use translucent material only for floating navigation and route readouts. Keep blur static, with an opaque fallback for reduced transparency. Device frames may use shadow to express physical depth.

## Shapes

Derive nested radii from the parent radius and padding. Keep shared panel geometry consistent across all six labs.

## Components

Use native buttons, radios, ranges, disclosures and a modal dialog. Every form control needs a visible label and keyboard focus. Preserve the drawer's opener when content changes and restore focus on dismissal.

Packet motion must explain direction or protocol behavior. Pause offscreen and hidden-page motion; respect reduced motion. Restrict animations to transform, opacity and small SVG packet strokes. Never animate layout dimensions or large blur surfaces.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Keep the three canonical seeds unchanged and accessible through the evidence register.
- Label brief-only figures, source conflicts, assumptions and proposed acceptance targets.
- Do not replace interaction with wide tables or long prose blocks.
- Do not introduce purple gradients, external runtime dependencies or sibling-project assets.
- Do not present simulated access, store-copy review or gate toggles as completed validation.
