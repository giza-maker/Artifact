# MAWJ mobile product direction

23 October 2026 redesign. Previous editorial direction was rejected. Public target: https://giza-maker.github.io/Artifact/mawj/.

Consumer mobile app for Gulf salon and wellness booking, presented to Saudi investors. Hero proves the app first; proposed commercial model follows. One signature composition: a tactile phone, salon photography, and a lime architectural backdrop. No autoplay phone carousel: visitors control the product.

## Tokens

- Paper #f6f7f2, ink/forest #19231e, muted #626d65, divider #dce0d7. Sole accent #d8f36a.
- Manrope variable 200–800, IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400/500, self-hosted WOFF2; font-display swap.
- Display 88px maximum desktop / 45–75px mobile; body 14–19px. App primary controls at least 44px, supporting labels minimum 10px.
- Container 1280px maximum, 48px desktop inset, 20px mobile. Hero copy and actual app; phones retain readable internal content and scroll.
- Surface radius 32px desktop / 26px mobile. Device radius derives from outer 48px and inset. Pills limited to actions and category selection.
- Official Tabler outline icons, single 24px family, local sprite.
- Motion: one-shot entry and intersection reveals (transform/opacity), press feedback, app screen transitions. No perpetual decorative loops, scroll events, blur animation, or layout animation. Reduced-motion bypasses nonessential movement.

## Grounding

Reused Kitchenaut mobile screen/back-navigation patterns and NileGate artifact-first responsive approach. See standards-sources.md for official interaction guidance and local asset provenance.

## Interaction contract

Complete app state: explore, search, categories, filters, saved, detail, service, professional, date, time, payment, confirmation, bookings, profile/reset. EN/AR synchronize between site and app; RTL is structural. Booking, reviews, prices and venues are fictional. No backend appointment or payment occurs.

Investor deck uses the established 12-slide evidence set; one interactive economics scenario; native modal focus/escape behavior. Partnership form downloads a local draft. Never imply actual lead capture or live app availability.

Maintain explicit idea-stage, proposed rates, target/traction and team-biography caveats. No invented testimonials, logos, partners, TAM or traction.
