## Copy boundaries

Primary review supplement: Celimax's [Korean 50 ml Dual Cream page](https://www.celimax.co.kr/product/%EB%A0%88%ED%8B%B0%EB%82%A0-%EC%83%B7-%ED%83%80%EC%9D%B4%ED%8A%B8%EB%8B%9D-%EB%93%80%EC%96%BC-%ED%81%AC%EB%A6%BC-50ml/415/category/1/display/2/) lists two formulations and confirms retinal, niacinamide and panthenol. No concentration or clinical-result claim is transferred into the UI. The French Mixa Cica page confirms urea, glycerin and niacinamide; its numerical study claims are excluded.

- No treatment claims for acne, eczema, pigmentation disorders, or other conditions.
- No efficacy percentages, study results, “anti-aging” promises, cure language, or authenticity claims.
- Product-name numbers such as 77, 100, PDRN, 10, and 4 are not independently interpreted unless manufacturer context explicitly supports a concentration; breakdown copy generally omits them.
- Ingredient cards show selected featured ingredients only. Local back-label INCI remains authoritative.
- Retinoid cautions stay general: introduce gradually, avoid stacking retinoids, avoid during pregnancy, and use daytime sunscreen.
- Price, stock, batch, expiry, importer, local notification, and local size uncertainty remain outside breakdown claims.
